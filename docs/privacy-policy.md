# 🔐 Nug Nug — Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 10, 2026

Nug Nug, also known as A.L.P.A.C.A. and Alfred Steven Bundy, is a Discord bot designed to assist with server moderation, security, role management, community interaction, and related server features.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Nug Nug may process and how that information is used.

## Information Nug Nug May Process

Depending on which features are enabled in a Discord server, Nug Nug may process:

- Discord user IDs
- Discord usernames and display names
- Server IDs
- Channel IDs
- Role IDs and role information
- Commands and interactions sent to Nug Nug
- Join and leave events
- Moderation and security events
- Messages in channels Nug Nug is permitted to access when message-based features are enabled
- Server configuration information
- Basic diagnostic, error, and operational logs

Nug Nug does not have access to private conversations or channels that Discord has not authorized the bot to access.

## How Information Is Used

Information may be processed to:

- Respond to commands and interactions
- Welcome or recognize server members
- Assist moderators and administrators
- Check or manage server roles
- Detect suspicious activity
- Enforce configured server rules
- Maintain server-specific settings
- Diagnose technical problems
- Improve reliability and security

## Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate Nug Nug, provide configured features, maintain security, resolve technical problems, or meet applicable legal requirements.

Temporary data and logs may be deleted automatically.

Server administrators may remove Nug Nug from their server at any time.

## Data Sharing

Personal information is not sold.

Information may be processed by hosting, database, artificial-intelligence, or infrastructure providers when necessary to operate Nug Nug.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, the service, or others from fraud, abuse, or security threats.

## Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect information processed or stored by Nug Nug.

However, no internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security.

## Discord

Nug Nug operates using Discord services and is also subject to Discord's applicable terms, policies, permissions, and platform limitations.

## Children's Privacy

Nug Nug is intended for use only by individuals who meet Discord's minimum age requirements.

## Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Nug Nug's features, infrastructure, or data practices change.

The Effective Date above will be updated when material changes are made.

## Contact

Questions, privacy requests, or deletion requests may be submitted through the contact method published by Nug Nug's operator.
